Bootcamp Description

Discover the operational architecture of this intensive innovation sprint structured by the School of Engineering and Applied Sciences.

Cross-Functional Teams

Students from different distinct specialties are combined into multidisciplinary squads to merge managerial and core engineering competencies.

Project-Based Learning

Each engineering cohort blueprints, provisions, and hosts a full web-based architectural MVP solving localized production bottlenecks.

Professional Review

Rigorous dual evaluation cycles handled thoroughly by internal academic chairs and invited external industry partners.

Agile Increments

Iterative delivery phases with integrated continuous critique updates to evolve early prototypes into secure production software.
